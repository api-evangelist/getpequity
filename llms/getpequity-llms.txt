# Pequity

> Pequity is a compensation management software platform that helps HR, compensation, and finance teams plan pay cycles, manage pay ranges, benchmark salaries, run approval workflows, and share employee compensation portals — combining spreadsheet familiarity with automation at scale. Backed by Norwest Venture Partners. No public developer API is currently published.
